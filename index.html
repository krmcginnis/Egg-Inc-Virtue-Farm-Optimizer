    Using the PC App

    Extract the ZIP. Double-click Start-Virtue-Optimizer.cmd for EID loading, or index.html for offline use. The launcher runs the local helper in the background and opens the app in your browser. EID import and updates do not need a visible command window. A brief launcher flash may appear. For troubleshooting, run Start-Virtue-Optimizer.cmd --console from a command prompt.

    Load your account using the sidebar EID field, then review Virtue Farm for current upgrades, artifacts, research, fuel, and flights. Continue to Planning to set goals and missions. Review Assumptions on How It Works before generating a plan.

    App Updates

    Use Update App in the sidebar to check for a new release. Update & Restart downloads and verifies the app, keeps your current farm and timeline, and reopens at the same local address. Saved farm files and browser settings are retained. Finish a search or account import before updating. If installation or startup fails, the previous app is restored. Updating requires the CMD launcher and a public GitHub release repository; Update Source can connect it once. No GitHub credentials are stored in the app.

    The Objective

    The planner searches for a short route from your current TE progress to a total TE target within one ascension. The solver compares research and sale timing and returns the fastest complete plan found. You can also specify a fixed route. Automatically Determine Truth Egg Allocation is checked by default. Uncheck it below the TE target to enter optional per-Virtue minimums. Checking it again retains your entered minimums and lets the solver allocate freely. Switches reset gems and incur the Soul Egg cost calculated from your remaining Soul Eggs and previous switch count.

    New TE remain pending until ascension. They do not increase earnings during this plan. The result stops when the target can be claimed; ascending and rebuilding afterward are outside this run.

    What the Simulator Includes

    • All 56 common researches, individual level prices, and tier unlock requirements.
    • Hab capacity, hover and Hyperloop effects, fleet size, train lengths, and physical purchase costs.
    • Fixed Epic Research, claimed TE, colleggtibles, artifacts, and socketed stones.
    • Monday 09:00–Tuesday 09:00: 2× earnings. Friday 09:00–Saturday 09:00: research cost ×0.30, in your selected event timezone.
    • Five separate lifetime delivery totals and 98 TE thresholds per Virtue.

    Purchase Timeline

    Purchase Timeline shows up to three fastest plans with different shift counts. Select a plan to view its summary, Quick Guide, purchase details, and walkthrough export. The fastest plan is selected initially. Save Plan retains all choices and the selected plan for replay. The search compares sale timing internally, and Search Details reports how many sales actually contain research purchases. Stop & keep best retains the best complete shift-count plans found before stopping. User Selected Sequence preserves your entered order.

    Shift summaries show the start above the end date/time and the highest modeled earning, shipping, and egg laying rates reached during each visit. Earnings use the selected earnings mode and weekly events; shipping and laying show farm capacity before fuel diversion. The same rates appear in the Quick Guide’s Shift Complete strip. Open a shift summary to see the quick guide: grouped purchases, individual research level ranges in game order, and each online or offline break with its duration and resume time. Breaks have labeled duration and resume boxes; each guide ends with one Shift Complete strip. Online waits under 10 seconds and purchase/switch interactions stay inside purchase groups. Open Full breakdown for individual actions, or Expand all for every layer. Existing saved plans automatically gain this view.

    Visit durations are determined by the solver. It compares earnings, fleet, capacity, and tier-unlock purchases with departing sooner, waiting for weekly events, and completing TE delivery. Automatic Planning compares different farm orders; User Selected Sequence preserves your entered order. Every C except the last permits research. With only one C, that visit is delivery-only using existing research. Final visits to every Virtue focus on delivery; necessary vehicle/hab upgrades, delivery gear, and configured ship missions are included when helpful or required. Each search starts from the entered farm. The bounded search retains competing purchase paths and reports the fastest complete plan found, not a proven global optimum. Stop & keep best retains completed alternatives.

    Import and Save

    Export walkthrough opens a PDF in a new browser tab. It includes compact shift summaries followed by the quick guide: purchase targets between breaks, research level ranges, break durations, and resume dates. Use Save PDF or the browser PDF controls to save or print it. It works offline. Save farm keeps all planning goals, including the route, per-Virtue targets, and the minimum offline break. New searches always use Balanced, including when loading older files with another effort setting. Older files use a 1-minute minimum break when none is saved. Save plan keeps a JSON copy that can be loaded and replayed.

    EID import queries Egg Inc.’s unofficial read-only backup endpoint through the local helper. It may fail if the service changes or your saved backup is stale. JSON backup import and manual inputs also work offline. EID is not retained in saved farm files.

    Pressing Enter in the sidebar EID field loads account upgrades, progress, fuel, existing flights, and artifact inventory. When the backup has an active Virtue farm, it also loads gems, common research, habs, vehicles, silos, and equipped gear and sets the plan start to now. Otherwise, account information still loads, with a notice at the top; your starting farm, gear, and start time are retained. An active ULTRA Pro subscription sets Video Doubler to Active (2×), including imports without a current Virtue farm. Other subscriptions retain your existing doubler setting. Planning goals and compatible alternate sets are retained. Account holds permanent bonuses and progress. Virtue Farm holds farm upgrades, tabbed Current/Earnings artifact sets, expandable Common Research, fuel, and flights. Calculated delivery gear appears in the results. Each group keeps its existing manual-edit setting. Planning holds goals, routes, timing, and planned ships. Its Starting Farm summary shows the current Virtue, claimed and pending TE, and last backup timestamp. Save Farm retains settings across all three pages. Epic Research and Colleggtibles start with compact summaries; open their details to review individual levels and bonuses. Enabling manual account editing opens these fields. Source labels distinguish imported values, retained data, manual overrides, and starting-farm artifact previews. Values edited and then locked remain labeled Manually Edited; reimport refreshes those values. Loaded account fields lock; a loaded farm also locks farm fields. Goals and planned alternate loadouts stay editable. Flights are read-only records; their launch fuel is already reflected in tank stock. Pending TE is calculated. JSON game-backup import follows the same automatic choice.

    Start from Scratch offers Undo Reset for the current app session, including unfinished input. The app opens with an empty farm. Load your saved farm or EID, or enter your current values before planning. Load farm also accepts saved plans and replays them.

    Assumptions

    Use the plan as a starting point; actual results may vary.

    Farm & Gear

    • Habs are always full. Estimates are most reliable around 100 TE or higher, when fill time is negligible.
    • Automatic sets use owned Virtue gear and stones. Research & Earnings scores income divided by the research cost multiplier, using laying and shipping limits. Income alternatives remain available; Delivery maximizes shipped eggs after solved research. Socketing/removal gem costs and future finds are excluded. Manual sets are used as entered.
    • Claimed TE affect earnings; pending TE do not until ascension. Lifetime deliveries below the milestone implied by claimed TE use that minimum.
    • Tier unlocks count purchased common-research levels in lower tiers. Epic Research is a fixed input and is not purchased by the solver.

    Earnings & Timing

    • No sleep hours are scheduled. Silo coverage is treated as continuously maintained; refills are extra check-ins, excluded from earning-break counts. Fresh farms start with one free silo. Maintain the selected video doubler and follow listed fueling steps.
    • No running-chicken bonus, boosts, drones, gifts, or mission rewards are included.
    • Offline breaks meet your minimum, at least 1 minute. The solver compares online waits and offline purchase batches. Total run time comes first; equal finish times favor fewer switches, then fewer breaks. Longer minimums can change run time.
    • Purchases and switch interactions earn at the online rate. Offline bonuses apply to the whole eligible break; weekly events apply at their boundaries.
    • Weekly events use your selected timezone: 2× earnings Monday 09:00–Tuesday 09:00; 70% research discount Friday 09:00–Saturday 09:00.

    Routes & Search

    • One ascension per plan. Start Next Ascension preserves permanent progress and starts at the previous finish time, without downtime.
    • Buy research on Curiosity, habs on Integrity, vehicles and cars on Kindness, silos on Resilience, and change artifacts on Humility. A fresh farm starts with the free Coop, Trike, and one silo. The current Virtue is prepended to a fixed sequence when needed.
    • Visit durations are chosen by the route-aware search. The final C is research-free; all final visits prioritize delivery. Final vehicle/hab upgrades are compared with completing without them.
    • Automatic Planning compares research and sale timing internally and displays the fastest complete plan found. To specify your own visits, enter a User Selected Sequence. Both modes use the same route-aware purchase and timing search.
    • The bounded search compares competing purchase paths and event-aligned departures. It has no guarantee of global optimality; saved plans never seed a new search.

    Ship Launches

    Choose missions separately for H1 and H2. The solver collects H1 fuel before the first visit, then refills on available visits before H2 as needed. Fuel without a refill visit is collected and reserved before H1. Unused fuel is retained whenever it fits; required tank-space adjustments are listed. Full diversion produces no gems or delivered TE. Humility fuel is produced on the farm alongside tank transfer. Mission slots and earlier returns are shared across both visits; final returns do not delay departure. FTL comes from Epic Research. Mission rewards and future artifact upgrades are not predicted. Maintain silos and return promptly for scheduled launches; special duration events are not predicted.

    Automatic Planning

    Set Maximum New Shifts as a ceiling. The solver compares route lengths and farm orders, including shorter routes, upgrade revisits, and delivery endings. Purchase Timeline shows up to three fastest complete plans with distinct shift counts. A lower count can rank first when it finishes faster; no plan is forced to spend the full allowance. Every final C is delivery-only. User Selected Sequence preserves your entered order and can stop early when the goal and missions are complete.
