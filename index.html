    Using the PC App

    Extract the ZIP. Double-click Start-Virtue-Optimizer.cmd for EID loading, or index.html for offline use. The launcher runs the local helper in the background and opens the app in your browser. EID import and updates do not need a visible command window. A brief launcher flash may appear. For troubleshooting, run Start-Virtue-Optimizer.cmd --console from a command prompt.

    Load your account on Account, then review Virtue Farm for current upgrades, artifacts, research, fuel and flights. Continue to Planning to set goals and missions. Review Assumptions on How It Works before generating a plan.

    App Updates

    Use Update App in the sidebar to check for a new release. Update & Restart downloads and verifies the app, keeps your current farm and timeline, and reopens at the same local address. Saved farm files and browser settings are retained. Finish a search or account import before updating. If installation or startup fails, the previous app is restored. Updating requires the CMD launcher and a public GitHub release repository; Update Source can connect it once. No GitHub credentials are stored in the app.

    The Objective

    The planner searches for a short route from your current TE progress to a total TE target within one ascension. You limit additional switches and let the planner choose repeated Virtue visits, or specify a fixed route. Per-Virtue minimums are optional. Switches reset cash and incur the Soul Egg cost calculated from your remaining Soul Eggs and previous switch count.

    New TE remain pending until ascension. They do not increase earnings during this plan. The result stops when the target can be claimed; ascending and rebuilding afterward are outside this run.

    What the Simulator Includes

    • All 56 common researches, individual level prices and tier unlock requirements.
    • Hab capacity, hover and Hyperloop effects, fleet size, train lengths and physical purchase costs.
    • Fixed Epic Research, claimed TE, colleggtibles, artifacts and socketed stones.
    • Monday 09:00–Tuesday 09:00: 2× earnings. Friday 09:00–Saturday 09:00: research cost ×0.30, in your selected event timezone.
    • Five separate lifetime delivery totals and 98 TE thresholds per Virtue.

    Assumptions

    Use the plan as a starting point; actual results may vary.

    Farm & Gear

    • Habs are always full. Estimates are most reliable around 100 TE or higher, when fill time is negligible.
    • Automatic sets use owned Virtue gear and stones. Research & Earnings scores income divided by the research cost multiplier, using laying and shipping limits. Income alternatives remain available; Delivery maximizes shipped eggs after solved research. Socketing/removal gem costs and future finds are excluded. Manual sets are used as entered.
    • Claimed TE affect earnings; pending TE do not until ascension. Lifetime deliveries below the milestone implied by claimed TE use that minimum.
    • Tier unlocks count purchased common-research levels in lower tiers. Epic Research is a fixed input and is not purchased by the solver.

    Earnings & Timing

    • No sleep hours are scheduled. Silo coverage is treated as continuously maintained; refills are extra check-ins, excluded from earning-break counts. Fresh farms start with one free silo. Maintain the selected video doubler and follow listed fueling steps.
    • No running-chicken bonus, boosts, drones, gifts or mission rewards are included.
    • Offline breaks meet your minimum, at least 1 minute. The solver compares online waits and offline purchase batches. Total run time comes first; equal finish times favor fewer breaks. Longer minimums can change run time.
    • Purchases and switch interactions earn at the online rate. Offline bonuses apply to the whole eligible break; weekly events apply at their boundaries.
    • Weekly events use your selected timezone: 2× earnings Monday 09:00–Tuesday 09:00; 70% research discount Friday 09:00–Saturday 09:00.

    Routes & Search

    • One ascension per plan. Start Next Ascension preserves permanent progress and starts at the previous finish time, without downtime.
    • Buy research on Curiosity, habs on Integrity, vehicles and cars on Kindness, silos on Resilience, and change artifacts on Humility. A fresh farm starts with the free Coop, Trike and one silo. The current Virtue is prepended to a fixed sequence when needed.
    • C1 and K1 default to 60-minute limits and can finish earlier. Each compatible staged search compares every opening-budget pair at fixed 30-minute intervals up to each maximum (300 minutes allowed); older files with between-interval limits must select a 30-minute step before a new search. Larger limits take longer.
    • Wasmegg optimized routes compare 1 through your maximum research sales and need 12 new switches from Curiosity, or 13 from another truth egg. Best Found uses free routing for smaller budgets. Search effort controls the additional free-routing search.
    • Staged comparisons preserve every smaller opening-budget option. Additional free routing is time-limited, so its result can vary when limits change. The search is heuristic, with no guarantee of global optimality; saved plans never seed a new search.

    Ship Launches

    Choose missions separately for H1 and H2. The solver collects H1 fuel before the first visit, then refills for H2 on K3, C4, I2 and R2 as needed. Unused fuel is retained whenever it fits; required tank-space adjustments are listed. Full diversion produces no gems or delivered TE. Humility fuel is produced on the farm alongside tank transfer. Mission slots and earlier returns are shared across both visits; final returns do not delay departure. FTL comes from Epic Research. Mission rewards and future artifact upgrades are not predicted. Maintain silos and return promptly for scheduled launches; special duration events are not predicted.

    Wasmegg Optimized Sequence

    C1: up to your configured time limit (default 60 minutes), targeting fleet size and Graviton Coupling. I1 precedes K1 if max Chicken Universes can finish in under one hour; otherwise K1 goes first. K1: best vehicles within your configured time limit (default 60 minutes). I1: max Chicken Universes. C2: fleet research and a four-hour Graviton Coupling attempt. K2: max vehicles and cars. R1: silos within one hour. C3: an optional delivery-research visit whose usefulness depends on the selected research-sale count. H1: best delivery loadout. K3: newly unlocked cars and Kindness TE. C4, I2, R2, H2: collect each remaining egg's TE share.
