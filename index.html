    Getting Started

    Extract the entire ZIP and double-click Start-Virtue-Optimizer.cmd. The launcher opens the app in your browser and runs the local helper in the background for account loading and updates. You can also open index.html for offline use with manual inputs or JSON files.

    1. Account: enter your Egg Inc. ID in the sidebar and press Enter, or select Load Farm to open a saved farm, plan, or game backup.
    2. Virtue Farm: review your current upgrades, research, artifacts, fuel, and flights.
    3. Planning: set your TE target, route, sleep hours, and ship missions. Check the plan start date and timezone.
    4. Purchase Timeline: select Find Fastest Plan, then review the resulting plan and Quick Guide.

    The app opens with an empty farm. Use Edit Account Manually or Edit Farm Manually to enter or correct values. Imported fields lock after loading; planning settings stay editable. Start from Scratch resets the inputs and timeline. Undo Reset restores them during the current session.

    Your Truth Egg Target

    Target Total Truth Eggs means claimed + pending TE across all five Virtues. New targets default to claimed TE + 40, up to 490, until you enter your own target. Pending TE become claimable at ascension and do not increase earnings during this run.

    Automatically Determine Truth Egg Allocation lets the planner choose how much to earn on each Virtue. Uncheck it to enter optional per-Virtue minimums. These are minimum final totals, not additional TE to earn. Rechecking automatic allocation retains your entries for later use.

    The plan covers one ascension and ends when the TE target and required launches are complete. Ascending and rebuilding afterward are outside its duration. Start Next Ascension prepares the next farm at the previous finish time with no added downtime; adjust that start if you ascend later.

    Routes and Research

    A shift is a switch to another Virtue. It resets gems and spends Soul Eggs based on your remaining Soul Eggs and previous switches. Automatic Planning compares farm orders and upgrade revisits within Maximum New Shifts. The default ceiling is 12; shorter plans can win.

    User Selected Sequence keeps your entered visit order, up to 30 new shifts. Use C for Curiosity, K for Kindness, I for Integrity, R for Resilience, and H for Humility. The current Virtue is added at the start when needed. A plan can finish early once its target and missions are complete.

    The last Curiosity visit is delivery-only. Research requires an earlier Curiosity visit; a route with only one Curiosity visit uses its existing research. Final visits to other Virtues can still include useful upgrades, artifact changes, and required launches. The planner compares buying more with departing sooner or waiting for a weekly event.

    The search returns the fastest complete plans found, rather than a mathematically proven global optimum. Every displayed plan is replayed against its saved inputs to check affordability, research prerequisites, Virtue permissions, switch costs, sleep constraints, fuel, launches, and the target. Failure to find a plan does not prove that none exists.

    Sleep, Waiting, and Dates

    No Game Interactions During Sleep is checked for new farms and after Start from Scratch, with Sleep Start at 23:00 and Wake Time at 07:00. Adjust these times to your schedule or uncheck it. Loaded files retain their saved sleep settings; older files without a sleep schedule keep sleep disabled so their timelines can replay.

    During enabled sleep hours, purchases, shifts, artifact changes, fuel setup, ship collection, and launches wait until you are awake. Ship travel and ongoing fuel transfers continue. Farm earnings, egg delivery, and farm-produced fuel continue while silos have coverage. Sleep uses offline earnings even when Online Only is selected.

    Refill silos before bed. When coverage runs out during sleep, farm production pauses until wake time. The planner compares legal, paid silo upgrades on Resilience with finishing sooner. Awake waits assume routine silo refills; those check-ins are not counted as earning breaks. Maintain the selected Video Doubler.

    Automatic Online + Offline compares online waits with offline earning breaks. Online Only uses online earnings outside sleep, without a running-chicken bonus. Minimum Offline Break sets the shortest voluntary offline earning break, with a minimum of 1 minute; scheduled sleep follows your sleep window. Seconds per Switch and Seconds per Purchase account for interaction time. The calibration multipliers adjust modeled earnings and common-research costs.

    Plan Start (PC Local Time) uses your PC clock. Sleep & Display Timezone controls sleep hours and displayed plan dates; Automatic uses your PC timezone. Sleep boundaries follow daylight saving changes. The TE target can be reached during covered sleep; claim pending TE when you are awake.

    Weekly Events

    Weekly events always use Pacific Time (America/Los_Angeles), following PST/PDT. Selecting a different sleep/display timezone does not move them.

    • Double earnings: Monday 09:00 through Tuesday 09:00.
    • Research sale: Friday 09:00 through Saturday 09:00, with common-research costs reduced by 70% (×0.30).

    Ships and Fuel

    Plan missions separately for H1 and H2, your first and second Humility visits. The planner includes launch costs, fueling, existing flights, and three shared mission slots. It collects required non-Humility fuel before the relevant visit and reserves H2 fuel early when the route has no later refill visit.

    Planning shows the required non-Humility tank space for each visit, including reserved H2 fuel. Surplus stored fuel may need to be discarded to make room; follow the timeline's tank-limit steps.

    Follow the listed fuel diversion and tank-limit steps. Full diversion produces fuel instead of gems or delivered eggs. Humility fuel is produced during its visit alongside tank transfers. Ships returning during sleep wait to be collected while you are awake. All required ships must be launched, but their final mission returns do not delay plan completion. Mission rewards, future artifact finds, and special mission-duration events are excluded.

    Reading the Purchase Timeline

    The timeline shows up to three complete plans with different shift counts, ordered by finish time. The fastest found is selected initially. Other cards show the extra time and shift difference compared with that fastest plan. Select another plan to see its summary, gear, Quick Guide, and purchase details. Stop & Keep Best retains completed alternatives found before stopping.

    Open a shift to view the Quick Guide: grouped purchases, research level ranges in game order, and waits with durations and resume times. Sleep and empty-silo time are included. Short online waits under 10 seconds stay within purchase groups. Full Breakdown shows individual actions; Expand All opens every layer.

    Shift summaries and Shift Complete strips show start/end dates with years and the highest modeled earning, shipping-capacity, egg-laying, and delivered-egg rates reached during each visit. Earnings reflect the selected waiting mode and weekly events. Delivered eggs use the lower of laying and shipping capacity: a fleet that can ship 3.098q/hour delivers 2.724q/hour if the farm lays 2.724q/hour. These peak rates are before fuel diversion and empty-silo pauses, not averages over the visit. Final delivered egg rate shows the farm's rate at plan completion. Search Details reports the search outcome and sales that actually included research purchases.

    A replay-verified result can be retained when rerunning the same inputs. A change that only improves starting earnings gear can also propose a purchase order, which must be paid and replayed with the new gear. Other input changes invalidate previous candidates. Changing sleep, dates, or farm values requires a new search; an existing timeline continues to show the inputs from its original run.

    Loading and Saving

    Save Farm saves the inputs across Account, Virtue Farm, and Planning. Save Plan also keeps the generated alternatives and selected timeline for replay. Load Farm accepts either JSON file as well as a game backup. Export Walkthrough opens a PDF with shift summaries and the Quick Guide; use Save PDF or your browser’s PDF controls to save or print it.

    Account loading reads Egg Inc.’s unofficial backup endpoint through the local helper. It makes no changes in the game. Review the backup timestamp: a stale backup may not reflect your current farm. If a current Virtue farm is found, loading refreshes its upgrades, equipped gear, gems, and plan start time. Otherwise, account bonuses load while the entered starting farm and start time are retained. Planning settings are kept in either case.

    An active ULTRA Pro subscription sets Video Doubler to Active (2×). Other subscription states keep your current selection. Existing flights are read-only records; their launch fuel has already been deducted from stored fuel. Pending TE is calculated from lifetime deliveries. The sidebar remembers your Egg Inc. ID locally; saved farm files do not include it.

    App Updates

    Select Update App → Update & Restart to install a new release. Finish or stop the current search or account import first. Updates verify the download, preserve your current inputs and timeline, and reopen the app at the same local address. Saved farm files and browser settings are retained. A failed installation or startup restores the previous app.

    Updates require the CMD launcher. Published releases already point to the app’s public GitHub repository; Update Source can connect a source checkout. No GitHub password or token is needed. For startup troubleshooting, run Start-Virtue-Optimizer.cmd --console from a command prompt.

    Assumptions

    Predicted times depend on these assumptions and the accuracy of your starting inputs.

    Farm and Gear

    • Habs are treated as full immediately. Below about 100 claimed TE, chicken fill time can make predicted completion too optimistic.
    • Automatic artifact sets use owned Virtue gear and stones. Research & Earnings balances income and research discounts; delivery sets balance laying and shipping after the plan’s upgrades. Set changes require Humility.
    • Manual sets are used as entered. Future artifact finds and socketing/removal gem costs are excluded. Equip the starting gear shown in the app before following the plan.
    • Claimed TE affect earnings; pending TE do not. Lifetime deliveries use at least the milestone implied by claimed TE.
    • Epic Research and Colleggtibles are fixed inputs. The planner does not buy Epic Research or earn new Colleggtibles.

    Earnings and Timing

    • Enabled sleep blocks game interactions and uses offline earnings. Production pauses when silos run empty during sleep; awake waits assume routine refills.
    • Maintain the selected Video Doubler. Running-chicken bonuses, boosts, drones, gifts, and mission rewards are excluded.
    • Purchases and shifts earn at the online rate. Eligible offline breaks use offline bonuses; weekly multipliers change at their event boundaries.
    • Weekly events stay at 09:00 Pacific, following PST/PDT. Your selected timezone controls sleep and displayed dates.

    Routes and Search

    • Research requires Curiosity, habs Integrity, vehicles/cars Kindness, silos Resilience, and artifact changes and launches Humility.
    • A fresh farm starts with the free Coop, Trike, and one silo. Switches reset gems and spend Soul Eggs.
    • The last Curiosity visit is research-free. Other final visits can include upgrades and required launches when useful.
    • Maximum New Shifts is a ceiling for Automatic Planning. User Selected Sequence preserves your order and can finish early.
    • The bounded search compares complete plans within a time budget. Replay validates a plan against the model; it does not prove that the plan is globally fastest.

    Ships and Fuel

    • Fuel diversion reduces gems and delivered eggs. Follow the listed collection, transfer, and tank-limit steps.
    • Existing flights and planned missions share slots across H1 and H2. Collections and launches require awake time; travel and ongoing transfers can continue during sleep.
    • Flight times use your Epic Research. Special duration events, mission rewards, and future gear are excluded. Final mission returns do not delay completion.
